Our Vision

To give SME manufacturers diagnostic precision over their production process — without the enterprise budget or the six-month IT project.

Our Mission

To turn existing operational data into stage-level clarity on where time, material, and energy are being lost — so every decision has a number behind it, not a theory.

Inshira was built on one observation from working with manufacturers: they track time, cost, and output — but when something goes wrong, they can't pinpoint which stage caused it or prove why. Decisions get made on instinct. Losses go unquantified for months. We build tools that change that. The operational fix comes first. The emissions reduction follows.

FAQs

Common questions from SME manufacturers.

Who is Inshira built for?
SME manufacturers — typically 20 to 500 employees — where production decisions are currently based on experience rather than stage-level evidence. Works best where the gap between what's tracked and what's understood is widest.
How is Inshira different from energy or carbon tools?
Those tools tell you what you've used. Inshira tells you where you're losing it and what it's worth fixing. Energy and carbon reduction are outcomes of fixing the production process, not separate reporting outputs.
Is Inshira a reporting tool?
No. Inshira tells you which stage is causing the problem, why, and what it's costing. Reports are an output, not the point.
Do we need ERP or MES systems?
No. Inshira works with the data you already collect. ERP and MES integration is available but not required to get started.
How long does it take to see results?
Weeks, not months. Designed for fast time-to-value without a lengthy implementation.
