About Us

Our Vision

To help SME manufacturers build resilience and reduce emissions through practical, circular manufacturing — where performance, circularity, and compliance reinforce each other.

Our Mission

To enable profitable decarbonisation by integrating energy efficiency, circular material flows, and compliance readiness into one decision platform for manufacturing teams.

The Inshira Story

Inshira comes from an Arabic word that means opening and expansion. This idea guides everything we build — opening practical pathways for industry and expanding what sustainable manufacturing can achieve.

We believe circularity becomes real when it is operational: when energy, materials, and waste are understood at the process level, and when by-products are engineered back into value. Inshira builds AI-enabled decision systems that help manufacturers reduce loss, recover value, and improve continuously.

At Inshira, our name is our operating manual — resolve, clarity, and relentless improvement in service of resilient and circular manufacturing.

FAQs

Common questions from SME manufacturers

Who is Inshira built for?

Inshira is built specifically for SME manufacturers operating under cost pressure and increasing sustainability and compliance requirements — not large enterprises with dedicated ESG or IT teams.

How is Inshira different from energy or carbon tools?

Most tools focus on a single issue. Inshira integrates energy efficiency, circular material flows, and compliance readiness into one system, reflecting how real manufacturing decisions are made.

Is Inshira a reporting or compliance tool?

No. Inshira is a decision platform that helps teams understand where to act first. Compliance outputs come from operational clarity, not manual reporting.

What data do we need to get started?

Only the data you already collect — such as energy usage, production volumes, material inputs, and waste records. No new sensors or complex IT systems are required to begin.

Do we need ERP or MES systems to use Inshira?

No. Inshira works as a standalone platform, using existing operational data such as spreadsheets, meters, and logs. The platform is built with an extensible architecture, enabling future integration with ERP and MES systems as needs evolve — while prioritising value delivery without long IT implementations today.
