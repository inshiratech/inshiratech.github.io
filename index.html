FAQs

Who is Inshira built for?
SME manufacturers, typically 20 to 500 employees, where decisions are based on experience rather than stage-level evidence.
How is Inshira different from energy or carbon tools?
Those tools tell you what you used. Inshira tells you where you are losing it and what it is worth fixing.
Is Inshira a reporting tool?
No. It shows which stage is causing the problem, why, and what it costs.
Do we need ERP or MES systems?
No. Inshira works with the data you already collect. Integration can come later.
How long does it take to see results?
Weeks, not months.
